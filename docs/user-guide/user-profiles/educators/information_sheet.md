# Research Participant Information Sheet

:::{div} text-center text-muted fs-5
The VirtualShip Team
<p>Freudenthal Institute, Faculty of Science, Utrecht University</p>
:::

---

## What is the purpose of the VirtualShip Classroom study?

Investigating the effectiveness of a computational virtual environment for fieldwork in higher education. Or specifically, investigating if students’ knowledge on sea-based research and related skills are increased by designing and simulating a virtual expedition and analysing the data collected.

## What will I do if I choose to be in this study?

You will use newly designed educational material during the course. To be part of the study you will fill in a survey at the end and the work you hand in might be analysed. You might be filmed if you watch the 360° videos.

## How long will I be in this study?

You will be part of the study for the preparation and duration of the VirtualShip tutorials and presentation, if applicable. Surveys should be filled in after the conclusion of all VirtualShip activities in your course and should take about 10 minutes to complete.

## What are the possible risks or discomforts?

Very little. During the 360°/VR experience you could experience minor nausea or dizziness.

## Are there any potential benefits to taking part in this study?

Enhanced satisfaction and learning.

## Will information about me and my participation in this study be kept confidential?

Your responses will be kept strictly confidential, and digital data will be anonymised and stored in secure computer files/storage. Any publications based on this research will not include your name or any other individual information by which you could be identified. The project's research records may be reviewed by departments at Utrecht University responsible for regulatory and research oversight. The information will be held in a secure research data management service up to 10 years and will be fully anonymised. In accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) you can have access to your information and can request your data to be deleted at any time during this period.

## What are my rights if I take part in this study?

Your participation in this study is voluntary. You will not be paid for your participation. You can withdraw your participation at any time without being penalised. Hence if desired, you can do the tutorials which are part of the course and skip the questionnaire and 360°/VR experience. If you withdraw your work will not be analysed.

## Who can I contact if I have questions about this study?

If you have questions, comments or concerns about this part of the course and/or research project, you can talk to one of the researchers of the VirtualShip Classroom or to the course coordinator. Please contact the VirtualShip Team ([virtualship@uu.nl](virtualship@uu.nl)) or Erik van Sebille ([e.vansebille.uu.nl](e.vansebille.uu.nl)).

If you have questions about your rights while taking part in the study or have concerns about the treatment of other articipants, please write to the secretary of the ethical commission at UU ([etc-beta-geo@uu.nl](etc-beta-geo@uu.nl)).

If you have questions or concerns about your privacy while taking part in the study, please send an email to: [privacy-beta@uu.nl](privacy-beta@uu.nl) or [privacy-geo@uu.nl](privacy-geo@uu.nl).
